The Python `cryptography` package is a runtime dependency, not a bundled copy. Its upstream license and dependency notices remain applicable. Its Ed25519 interface is invoked through a transparent local wrapper. Its algorithms are not claimed as an original contribution.

The accompanying paper, distributed separately from this repository, uses the supplied unmodified IEEEtran class and an unmodified normal IEEEtran bibliography style. Their upstream copyright and LaTeX Project Public License notices are preserved in those files. They are not relicensed by this repository's MIT license.

The exact scholarly and official resources, acquisition status and integration scope are listed in `external_resources.csv`. Access to a paper through the web reader is not permission to redistribute its PDF. Unverified redistribution rights are labeled accordingly.
