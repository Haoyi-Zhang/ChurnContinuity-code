# Claim Language Guide

Normative wording used throughout the submission:

- **validates**: a deterministic verifier accepts a syntactic, signature, subgroup, opening, or algebraic relation;
- **establishes under assumptions**: a theorem derives a semantic property from explicit premises;
- **checks exhaustively in the finite model**: every state or assignment in the documented finite domain was enumerated;
- **measures**: an experiment reports an observed quantity on the stated host and workload;
- **does not claim**: a nearby stronger interpretation is outside scope.

Forbidden substitutions include “publicly proves plaintext equality” for commitment consistency, “exactly once” without the identifier and cut premises, “Byzantine private” for protocol-following view privacy, and “production ready” for a research prototype.
